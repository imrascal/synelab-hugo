Congrats to Zihan for his co-first authored paper (likely the best graduation gift) "Unveiling Spatiotemporal Diffusion of Hot Carriers Influenced by Spatial Nonuniform Hot Phonon Bottleneck Effect in Monolayer MoS2" being published in Nano Letters.

We collaborated with Prof. Weimin Liu in exploring the hot carrier diffusion dynamics in monolayer MoS2 by transient absorption microscopy. We found the anomalous "negative diffusion" phenomenon and experimentallly demonstrated its physical origin governed by the hot phonon bottleneck (HPB) effect.
