## About

B.S. (2007-2011) and Ph.D. (2011-2016) in Chemistry from Peking University; Postdoctoral Fellow in Electrical Engineering at MIT (2016-2020). Joined the School of Physical Science and Technology (SPST) at ShanghaiTech University as a Principal Investigator and Researcher in January 2021, dedicated to realizing next-generation low-dimensional electronics through chemical approaches.
