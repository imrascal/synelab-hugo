Congrats to Chenyu for her co-first authored paper entitled "Large-Substrate-Terrace Confined Growth of Arrayed Ultrathin PtSe2 Ribbons on Step-Bunched Vicinal Au(001) Facets Toward Electrocatalytic Applications" being published in Small.

We collaborated with Prof. Yanfeng Zhang's group in building up a concise theretical model for understanding the growth mode switching between terrace confinement and step overflow.
