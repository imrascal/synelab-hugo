title: Electrically measuring lead-based perovskites
date: 2024-02-13
tag: research
---

We in collaboration with Prof. Zhijun Ning's group have developed the electrical measurement strategy to probe the carrier polarity and density of lead-based perovskites, which typically manifest severe hysteresis in the transfer characteristics. Click [here](https://www.x-mol.com/groups/SynE/news/60410) for more information.
